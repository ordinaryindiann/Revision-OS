Spaced Repetition Curves & Subject Cycles

Customize your 9-step day intervals (R1 to R9) or assign distinct curves per subject when Mode 2 is active.

How the cycles work

Each revision interval is measured from the last completed revision. Short gaps reinforce fresh material; longer gaps test durable recall.

Standard Spaced: Balanced reinforcement for steady preparation — 1, 3, 7, 14, 30, 45, 60, 90, 120 days.
Intensive Sprint: Shorter gaps for high-priority topics or exam-season consolidation — 1, 2, 4, 7, 12, 20, 30, 45, 60 days.
Long-Term Retention: Wider intervals for material you already recall reasonably well — 2, 5, 12, 25, 45, 75, 110, 150, 180 days.
R1–R9: Nine planned passes. Mark a pass only after completing recall/review; the next due date is calculated from the chosen curve.
